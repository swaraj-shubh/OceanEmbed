    Bias Correction (Calibration)

    Calibrated on 2022 Argo validation period

    Eliminating systematic depth-dependent warm bias inherited from the teacher model (GLORYS) before deployment on test data.

    1 · Fit per-depth bias on 2022 Argo

    bd = mean( Ŷens,d − TdArgo )

    over all valid 2022 profiles, for each depth d

    2 · Apply to test period (2023–2024, unchanged)

    Ŷfinal,d = Ŷens,d − bd
