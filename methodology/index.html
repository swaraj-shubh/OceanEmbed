    Seeing 1,000 m below the surface — from space.

    Satellites only see the top of the ocean. OTER (Ocean Thermal Embedding Reconstruction) learns what that surface is telling us about the water beneath it, and rebuilds the temperature at 15 depths, every day, over the Arabian Sea and the Bay of Bengal.

    7 satellite maps in 15 depths out · 0–1000 m 0.25° (~28 km) · daily checked against real Argo floats
    Dive in

    The problem

    Two ways to look at the ocean. Neither is enough alone.

    Satellites

    See everywhere, every day — but only the thin surface layer.

    16,896grid cells observed every single day
    OTER learns the link between the two

    Argo floats

    Measure all the way down — but they are few and far between.

    —deep profiles in the whole region, within 5 days of 4 Dec 2023

    So we train a model on years of surface maps paired with full-depth temperatures, then ask it to fill in the ocean below — everywhere, every day. And we grade it on the real floats, which it never saw.

    4Model Architecture · OceanEmbed M4

    Read · remember · paint

    7 daily inputs → CNN Encoder → ConvLSTM → U-Net Decoder → 15-depth output. Tap any component or depth slab to inspect its details.

    Interactive Diagram Click or tap any component or depth slab to inspect details
    ① CNN encoder

    Spatial feature extraction

    Extracts multi-scale spatial patterns from 7 surface fields across the 7-day window.

    ① CNN encoder — read

    Shared convolutional feature extraction

    Shared across time. Four downsampling stages: 7 → 32 (96×176), 32 → 64 (48×88), 64 → 128 (24×44), and 128 → 256 (12×22). Compresses spatial maps while expanding channel abstractions like fronts and eddies.

    ② ConvLSTM — remember

    Spatiotemporal recurrent latent memory

    Steps through 7 recurrent temporal steps. Its final hidden state (ht) forms a compact 256 × 12 × 22 tensor: the OceanEmbed spatiotemporal latent fingerprint summarizing subsurface thermal memory.

    ③ U-Net decoder — paint

    Reconstruction with skip connections

    Upsamples the latent fingerprint across 128 (24×44) → 64 (48×88) → 32 (96×176) → 15 depths (96×176). Three direct skip connections carry fine spatial gradients straight across, preserving sharp coastlines and fronts.

    6Training · Two Loss Variants

    Learning from GLORYS12V1 with masked loss

    The model learns from the GLORYS12V1 ocean reanalysis target using masked supervision to avoid land and bathymetry artifacts.

    TRAIN (2015–2021): Fitting network weights
    VALIDATION (2022): Calibrating per-depth bias correction only
    TEST (2023–2024): Final independent evaluation

    Masked Loss Computation

    L = 1/Nvalid ∑i ∈ valid wdepth(i) (Ŷi − Yi)2
    • Land points: Masked out across all inputs and targets
    • Missing values: Mask missing GLORYS cells
    • Seafloor bathymetry: Computed only on valid (depth, lat, lon) ocean cells

    At 100 m, shallow continental shelves (hatched) have no water and are excluded from the loss.

    Two Loss Variants

    wdepth = 1 for M4-MSE. Inverse-variance weights wdepth = 1 / σ²depth (from training set) for M4-DW ensure calm deep waters and energetic thermocline contribute balanced gradients.

    8Bias Correction (Calibration)

    Calibrated on 2022 Argo validation period

    Eliminating systematic depth-dependent warm bias inherited from the teacher model (GLORYS) before deployment on test data.

    1 · Fit per-depth bias on 2022 Argo

    bd = mean( Ŷens,d − TdArgo )

    over all valid 2022 profiles, for each depth d

    2 · Apply to test period (2023–2024, unchanged)

    Ŷfinal,d = Ŷens,d − bd

    Fixed 15 calibration values subtracted from every test day — zero future leakage.

    Evaluation Procedure

    1 · Match

    Match each Argo profile to nearest model calendar day (±1 day).

    2 · Extract

    Extract model prediction at float coordinates (nearest 0.25° cell).

    3 · Interpolate

    Resample float readings to the 15 standard model depths.

    4 · Filter

    Compare only at valid observed depths (where Argo exists).

    5 · Metrics

    Compute RMSE, MAE, Bias, Corr & R² per depth and blended.

    Important Principles (No Data Leakage)

    ✓Argo is never an input to the model.
    ✓GLORYS is used only as the training target.
    ✓Normalization uses TRAIN stats only.
