OceanEmbed — Documentation Index (SIH PS 26066)

Reconstruction of subsurface ocean temperature (15 depths, 0–1000 m) from 7 surface satellite fields over the Arabian Sea + Bay of Bengal. Team constitution: ../CLAUDE.md.
